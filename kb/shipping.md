# Shipping

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days within the EU. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 9 EUR. Orders placed before 14:00 on a business day leave the warehouse the same day.

## Shipping costs
Standard delivery costs 4 EUR. It is free for orders of 50 EUR or more.

## Late or lost parcels
If a parcel has not arrived 7 business days after shipping, the customer should open a support ticket. We then trace it with the carrier and send a replacement or refund if it is lost.
