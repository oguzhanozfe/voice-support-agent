# Account and orders

## Changing a delivery address
An address change applies only to orders that have not shipped yet. An order with the status shipped goes to the address it was shipped to, so the customer has to contact the carrier or open a support ticket.

## Cancelling an order
An order can be cancelled only while its status is processing. Cancellations are handled by a human agent.

## Opening hours
Human support agents work Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 18:00 Central European Time. Requests outside these hours are answered on the next business day.
