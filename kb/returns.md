# Returns

## Return window
Customers can return most items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

## Items that cannot be returned
Opened headphones and earbuds cannot be returned for hygiene reasons unless they are faulty. Gift cards cannot be returned.

## Return shipping
Return shipping is free when the item is faulty or wrong. In all other cases the customer pays a flat return fee of 5 EUR.

## Refund timing
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after the returned item reaches the warehouse.
